# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

PMPC employees record attendance, review their personal employment information, and access tasks and payslips. PMPC super administrators manage people, attendance corrections, payroll, reports, and system settings.

## Product Purpose

PMPC WorkForce keeps daily attendance, employee administration, and payroll operations in one role-aware workforce system.

## Operating Context

Employees use the system throughout a workday, especially to complete the four-step DTR sequence. Administrators use it for recurring HR, attendance, and payroll operations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Laravel with Inertia and React. Existing URLs, role protections, DTR rules, payroll calculations, and server-side forms remain unchanged during frontend work.

## Brand Commitments

Use the existing PMPC logo. Employee surfaces use PMPC green; administrator surfaces use deep purple. The default theme is light, with an optional dark theme.

## Product Principles

- Make the next operational action obvious.
- Keep attendance, people, and payroll information legible at a glance.
- Preserve familiar forms and dependable role-based workflows.
- Design for desktop operations and mobile employee self-service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, responsive layouts, and WCAG-aware contrast in both themes.
